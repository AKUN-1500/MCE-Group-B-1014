2026 ICID MCE • SLIDE 02

Self-Guided Story Trips Guided by Local AI Characters

01 / Initial Concept 2–4 Hour Experience

Neighborhood Story Walks Guided by AI Characters

A short, self-guided trip through one neighborhood designed like stepping into an interactive story. Real partner venues (cafés, bookstores, boutique hotels) serve as story “scenes,” with dynamic plots evolving based on user decisions.

02 / Target Audience User Persona

Time-poor Urban Young Adults & Curious City Explorers

Individuals seeking spontaneous weekend micro-escapes and immersive cultural discovery beyond generic tourist itineraries, requiring zero planning overhead.

03 / Proposed Offering Service & Technology

• Choose-your-own-adventure routes connecting local retail partners as narrative checkpoints.
• Physical & digital keepsakes collected at each venue to commemorate the story.
• Zero-download Web-App: Instant QR onboarding for frictionless, instant engagement.

04 / Key Question to Explore Next Core Challenge

“Will travelers form genuine emotional attachment to an AI brand character, and what persona design keeps it engaging rather than feeling like a promotional sales bot?”
